# News and Events

- **May 2025** - 10,813,057 occurrence records supplied by 172 different data providers have been integrated into MyCoPortal.

- **August 2020** - GLOBAL Bryophyte & Lichen Thematic Collections Network (TCN) [NSF Press Release](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2001422){:target="_blank"} [Project website](https://globaltcn.utk.edu/){:target="_blank"}

- **2018** - [fdex:](https://www.fdex.org){:target="_blank"} a simplified names database, primarily for projecting taxonomic hierarchy for fungal taxa.

- **August 2015** - [NSF Press Release](https://web.archive.org/web/20250110090736/https://nsf.gov/news/news_summ.jsp?cntn_id=136007&org=NSF&from=news){:target="_blank"} (#15-092) - NSF awards fifth round of grants to enhance America's biodiversity collections. The Microfungi Collections Consortium: A Networked Approach to Digitizing Small Fungi with Large Impacts on the Function and Health of Ecosystems ([NSF ADBC 1502735](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=1502735){:target="_blank"}).

- **May 2015** - [Microfungi Collections Consortium (MiCC)](https://www.microfungi.org/){:target="_blank"} website now live

- **April 2012** - [NSF Press Release](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=1206197){:target="_blank"} (#12-082) - US National Science Foundation awards support for The Macrofungi Collection Consortium, a collaboration of 35 institutions in 24 states for the purpose of databasing some 1.4 million dried scientific specimens of macrofungi ([NSF ADBC 1206197](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=1206197){:target="_blank"}).

- **December 2013** - 1,546,358 occurrence records supplied by 31 different data providers have been integrated into MyCoPortal.

- **2013** - MaCC records are now part of the Zooniverse project *Notes from Nature*.
